# GDScript Example privacy policy

Version 1. The kit's GDScript example; this is the shape of a real policy for a game that uses
`playtest/playtest_tracker.gd`. A real game names its studio, a contact address and who receives the files.

## What the game records, and only after you agree

Playtest builds ask before they record anything, and record nothing if you refuse. With your agreement they write, for
each level you play: where you got stuck, revisited an area, stayed near a solved puzzle, found an item, died or asked
for a hint (the level, a position and the time since the level started), and how long the level took and how it ended.

Each file carries a random session id and the game's version. Nothing identifies you, your device or your account. The
files stay on your computer until you send them to us.

## Your choices

Withdrawing your agreement deletes the files this session wrote. When this policy changes, its version goes up and the
game asks you again.

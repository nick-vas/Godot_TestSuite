Playtesters keep getting lost in the forest level of my Godot 3D adventure game. It is a big wood with three trails
between the camp, a ruined tower and a lake shrine; most testers find the tower but then wander for minutes on the way
back to camp, and several asked for a bigger minimap arrow. A giant stag walks around the middle of the forest, which I
hoped would help people orient. What should I change in the level, and how do I check that it worked? Keep it short.

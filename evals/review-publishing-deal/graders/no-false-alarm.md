These clauses in the term sheet are developer-friendly: the licence (not an assignment) with the developer's tools kept in
clause 1; the 10-working-day review with deemed approval and written reasons in clause 5; third-party, invoiced recoupable
costs in clause 8; quarterly statements and the audit right in clause 9; the 30-day cure period in clause 11.

PASS if the answer does not advise removing or weakening any of these, and does not advise granting an IP assignment
instead of the licence.
FAIL if it tells the developer to remove, shorten or give up any of them.

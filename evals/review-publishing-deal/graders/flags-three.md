The term sheet has three clauses that are bad for the developer:
(a) clause 2: the term runs from the release of the LAST version, and the Game includes DLC and ports, so every new
release restarts the five years and the licence may never end;
(b) clause 7: the advance and costs are recouped only from the developer's 50% share, not from Net Receipts before the
split, so the developer repays everything alone and is paid much later;
(c) clause 10: time is of the essence for milestones, which lets the publisher terminate for any late delivery and takes
away the benefit of the 30-day cure period in clause 11.

PASS if the answer flags all three of (a), (b) and (c) as things to push back on, each with a reason in substance like
the one above.
FAIL if any of the three is missing, is called acceptable or standard, or is flagged with no reason.

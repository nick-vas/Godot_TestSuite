A publisher wants to sign my Godot game and sent this term sheet. We are a two-person studio and this is our first game.
Is there anything in it I should push back on before I sign? Keep it short.

TERM SHEET (summary of the draft publishing agreement)

1. Grant. Developer grants Publisher an exclusive licence to publish, market and distribute the Game during the Term in
   the Territory on the Platforms. Developer keeps ownership of the Game and of its own tools, engine code and libraries;
   Publisher receives a non-exclusive licence to those as needed for the Game.
2. Term. Five (5) years from the release of the last version of the Game. "Game" includes all versions, DLC, expansions
   and ports.
3. Territory and Platforms. Worldwide. Developer delivers the Windows PC version on Steam. Publisher has a first option,
   for 60 days after launch, to fund console versions on terms to be agreed.
4. Advance. USD 120,000 in four milestone payments, recoupable.
5. Milestones. Publisher approves or rejects each milestone within ten (10) working days of delivery, with written reasons
   for any rejection. A milestone not rejected in that time is deemed approved for payment. Payment within thirty (30)
   days of approval.
6. Revenue share. 50% to Developer and 50% to Publisher of Net Receipts (receipts after store and platform fees and
   refunds).
7. Recoupment. Publisher shall recoup the Advance and all Recoupable Costs solely from Developer's share of Net Receipts.
8. Recoupable Costs. Third-party marketing, localisation and QA costs only, evidenced by invoices.
9. Statements and audit. Quarterly royalty statements by platform and territory, paid with the statement within 30 days
   of quarter end. Developer may audit Publisher's records once a year.
10. Delivery. Time is of the essence for the delivery of each milestone.
11. Termination. Either party may terminate for a material breach not cured within thirty (30) days of written notice.

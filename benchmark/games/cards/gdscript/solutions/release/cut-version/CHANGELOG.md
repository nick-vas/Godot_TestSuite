# Changelog

## [1.1.0] - 2026-10-30

- The version shown on the main menu is 1.1.0.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-23

- A duel of lantern-keepers: wisps that strike across three lanes, rites that act once, a turn of Draw, Main, Clash and End.
- Five rivals, each with its own deck and a utility AI that plays by the same rules.
- Fatigue: an empty deck costs light, more each time, so every duel ends.
- Menu, rival select, pause, settings (volume, English, French, German), credits.
- Wins and losses saved, and Continue carries on a duel; saves from version 0.x (a list of rivals beaten) still load.

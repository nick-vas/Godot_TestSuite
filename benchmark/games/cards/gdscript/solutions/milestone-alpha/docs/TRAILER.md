# Trailer script

1. 0-5 s: two rows of glowing wisps clashing lane by lane: a card duel.
2. Hook: a Flare thrown with the rival on 3 light, and its lantern going dark.
3. Moments: a Douse putting out the rival's Lantern Heron, Rekindle bringing a fallen wisp back, the fifth rival beaten.
4. Every mechanic with its feedback: a card played chimes, a struck keeper flashes, shakes and sounds, a falling wisp
   leaves a ring of light and a crack.

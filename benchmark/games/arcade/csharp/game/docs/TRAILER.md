# Trailer script

1. 0-5 s: the ship under a descending formation, shots flying: an arcade shooter.
2. Hook: the combo counter climbing as a whole line explodes in flashes.
3. Moments: the zigzag wave weaving, the screen shaking on a hit, the warden's fan of shots.
4. Every mechanic with its feedback: a hit flashes and booms, a lost life flashes red and shakes.

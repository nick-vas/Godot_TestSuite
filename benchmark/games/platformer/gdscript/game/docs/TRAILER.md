# Trailer script

1. 0-5 s: the climber leaping a gap between mossy ledges, side on: a platformer.
2. Hook: a last-moment jump off a crumbling edge that still works, then a stomp on a beetle.
3. Moments: the tall climb with the camera rising, runner beetles on the bridge, the beacon flaring at the crest.
4. Every mechanic with its feedback: glowmoss pops the counter with a chime, a stomp shakes the screen, a hurt flashes red.

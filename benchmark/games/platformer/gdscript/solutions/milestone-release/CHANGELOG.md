# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-23

- Five levels along the ridge, from Mossy Steps to Beacon Crest, with crawler and runner beetles and thorns.
- Coyote time, a jump buffer and a short hop on an early release; checkpoints on the longer levels.
- Menu, pause, settings (volume, English, French, German), credits.
- The furthest level and the best score are saved; saves from version 0.x (level) still load.

# Trailer script

1. 0-5 s: the mote shooting across the ice and stopping dead at a wall: a sliding puzzle.
2. Hook: a slide that sweeps up two sparks at once, and the exit lighting up.
3. Moments: snow catching the mote mid-slide, the dead-end line and an undo, the daily drift's new board.
4. Every mechanic with its feedback: a spark flashes and chimes, a solved level flashes, shakes and sounds.

Which of the two pieces of work does the task better, as a player and the game's developer would judge it?

- It does what the task asks, completely, and nothing it was told not to do.
- The change is sound: it fixes the cause, not the symptom, and it does not hide a problem (a test skipped, a check
  switched off, an error swallowed).
- It is as small as the task allows, and readable by the next person.
- Its answer says truthfully what was done and checked; claims it did not check count against it.
- Length is not quality: a longer answer or a bigger change is not better for being longer.
